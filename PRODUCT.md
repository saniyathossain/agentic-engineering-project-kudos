# Product

## Platform
web (responsive: 375 px phone to wide desktop)

## Users
- **Owner**: a founder, freelancer or small-team marketer who wants social proof on their site without a heavy tool. Checks the inbox between tasks, often on a phone.
- **Respondent**: a customer who opens a link, writes two sentences and leaves. No account, no friction, must feel safe and quick.

## Purpose
Collect testimonials through one themed link, curate them in an inbox, and show the best ones on any site with one iframe. Free to start, Pro to lift limits.

## Voice
Plain, warm, specific. Say what happened and what to do next. No hype, no exclamation marks, no emoji in the product, no "unlock", "supercharge", "seamless".

## Principles
1. The respondent's page is the product's face: fast, calm, trustworthy.
2. The inbox answers "what needs me?" first (pending count), detail second.
3. Quotes are the content. Give them room and a serif voice; keep chrome quiet.
4. Never expose a respondent's email outside the owner's inbox.

## Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, full keyboard use, labelled controls, visible focus, reduced motion and reduced transparency honoured, works at 375 px, light and dark equal in quality.
